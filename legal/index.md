## Simple Strategy: Just Don’t Break the Law

Rare Digits respects lawyers but prefers not to give them reasons to send invoices. To achieve this, the company employs an ingenious lifehack in all its operations: it aims to comply with applicable laws and to honor the commitments it makes.

Visitors are expected to follow similar principles and to comply, as Rare Digits does, with the [Terms of Use](/legal/terms/), which govern the relationship between this website and its visitors.

## A Conversation Before a Lawsuit

Anyone who feels the need to discuss legal matters with Rare Digits is invited to settle them pre-trial first. It is consistently more beneficial, cost-effective, and faster for everyone involved.

## UAE-Based, Globally Minded

As a friendly reminder, Rare Digits is legally established in the UAE and complies with all local regulations. Anyone who believes the company has somehow failed to meet their local legal expectations is welcome to say so. [A cookie is available in the meantime](/legal/privacy/) 🍪

Legal questions can be sent to [legal@raredigits.io](mailto:legal@raredigits.io).
