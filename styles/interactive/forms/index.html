Principles

Tips

Stop thinking about decorations, start thinking about the value.

The only thing that matters is the data your audience came for. Design, layouts, make-up and decorations are just your tools to highlight the value.

Imagine you have 30 secs to pitch your data. What would you say? What decision maker need to know? How would you present it? What would you highlight? What would you hide?

That's it. That's the only thing that matters. Delete the noise. Make your audience satisfied.

Interactive Elements / Forms

css/modules/elements/_forms.scss

Form fields need no class. Inside .rd, on a page or in an island, the plain elements take the library's look; a host form outside .rd never does. The library adds a frame, inner spacing, a 1rem text size and the states below. A field and a button of the default size are equally tall, so they line up in a row. The field keeps small corners (--field-radius, 4px) next to the button’s capsule on purpose: the field is a surface to type into, the button an action.

Text fields, textarea, select

The text fields are input without a type and the types text, email, password, search, tel, url and number. The date and time types (date, time, datetime-local, month, week) take the same frame and height; the browser draws their segments and picker inside it. The types submit, button and reset are buttons. A placeholder is --text-color-light (5.7:1 on white; the browser's own grey is 2.3:1). A textarea grows downwards only. A select draws its own arrow in the text colour; a list box (multiple or size) keeps the browser's. The arrow is a background image under appearance: none: a page rule that sets the background shorthand on a select clears the arrow and leaves no affordance. Set background-color only, or add appearance: auto to keep the native control.

Checkbox and radio

Drawn by the library (appearance: none): a 1em box that sits on the text line like a glyph, filled with --field-accent when checked. Wrap the control in its <label>: the whole line becomes the hit area. A checkbox set to indeterminate by script shows a bar.

<label><input type="checkbox" name="news"> Release notes</label>
<label><input type="radio" name="plan" checked> Monthly</label>

Range, colour, file

The range is a 2px line in the text colour with a round thumb framed in the same colour, drawn by the library in every browser. The range and the colour well are --space-lg tall, not tied to the height of a text field; the well is a square. The file input is a frame across the full width of its container, with no fill: its small button (.button-sm) and the file name sit inside it.

Groups, rows, help and errors

.form-group
One field: its label, the control, a help or error line, stacked. The fields take the group's width; a checkbox, a radio or a button keeps its own.
.form-row
Groups and a button side by side, their bottoms aligned so the button lines up with the fields. A bare field in the row grows too, so a one-line form, a field and its button, takes the full width: <form class="form-row"><input …><button>…</button></form>. Stacked on a phone.
The submit button
A type="submit" button (or a <button> with no type) in a .form-group or a .form-row sits at the right edge: on the fields' line, on a line of its own when the row wraps, and when the row stacks on a phone. To put it elsewhere, add .margin-l-0 and .self-start (or -center, -stretch).
.form-help, .form-error
Small lines under a control. An error is in the body text colour and marked with the error glyph in --field-invalid-color: red text would read at 4:1, short of AA. Tie both to the control with aria-describedby.
Shipping
As it appears on the door.
Enter a city. 
<div class="form-group">
    <label for="city">City</label>
    <input id="city" type="text" aria-invalid="true" aria-describedby="city-error">
    <span id="city-error" class="form-error">Enter a city.</span>
</div>

A <fieldset> gets a light frame and may shrink with its column; it stacks its groups and rows with a --space-md gap, since .form-group and .form-row carry no margins of their own; its <legend> is bold.

States

Hover, focus
The frame darkens under the pointer and turns to --field-accent on focus, inside the library's focus ring.
disabled
A grey field with grey text and the default pointer. The label of a disabled checkbox or radio dims with it.
Invalid, valid
Only after the user has had a go: :user-invalid and :user-valid match once a field was edited or its form submitted, so an untouched form shows no errors. The frame turns --field-invalid-color; a field that asked for something (required, pattern) turns --field-valid-color once it is right. A script marks a field with aria-invalid="true", which shows at once.

Tokens

TokenDefaultSets
--field-bg / --field-textvar(--white) / var(--primary-color)Fill and text of a field.
--field-border-colorvar(--gray-trans)The frame: 3.5:1 on white, the 3:1 a field's outline needs (WCAG 1.4.11).
--field-radiusvar(--space-xs)Corner radius; a checkbox takes half.
--field-padding-x / -ycalc(var(--space-sm) * 1.5) (12px) / var(--space-sm)Inner spacing; the default button takes -y too, so a field and a button stay equally tall when you change it.
--field-accentvar(--signal)Checked boxes and radios, the focused frame.
--field-invalid-colorvar(--fail)The frame of an invalid field and the error glyph.
--field-valid-colorvar(--success)The frame of a required field once it is right.

Inside a chart

A RareCharts card (.rc-chart) owns its controls, so these defaults stop at it. The page's own checkboxes and selects that a chart moves into its controls row keep the chart's compact look.
