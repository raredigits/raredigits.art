The Value

Principles

Tips

Stop thinking about decorations, start thinking about the value.

The only thing that matters is the data your audience came for. Design, layouts, make-up and decorations are just your tools to highlight the value.

Imagine you have 30 secs to pitch your data. What would you say? What decision maker need to know? How would you present it? What would you highlight? What would you hide?

That's it. That's the only thing that matters. Delete the noise. Make your audience satisfied.

Colors / Legend

css/modules/bricks/_legend.scss

A legend is the key to a chart or to any colour-coded view — a status map, a coloured table, a timeline. .legend is a wrapping row of .legend__items; each item is a swatch and a label. The swatches have the shapes and sizes of the legend RareCharts draws inside a chart, so a legend on the page and a legend in a chart read as one.

  • Revenue
  • Forecast
  • Orders
  • Plan range
<ul class="legend">
    <li class="legend__item">
        <span class="legend__swatch legend__swatch--line" style="--swatch-color: #1677ff"></span>
        Revenue
    </li>
</ul>

Swatches

The colour comes from --swatch-color, set on the swatch, on the item or on the whole legend; without it a swatch takes the text colour. The bare .legend__swatch is a small square; a modifier gives it the shape of the series it names.

.legend__swatch--line
A short rule, for a line series.
.legend__swatch--dashed
A dashed rule, for a forecast or a projected series.
.legend__swatch--dot
A dot, for bars, slices and points.
.legend__swatch--band
A softened square, for a shaded range: a confidence interval, an envelope.

Toggles

When the legend switches series on and off, each item is a <button> with aria-pressed. The legend drops the button's chrome, so the item still reads as a label, and aria-pressed="false" dims it as the off state. The page's script toggles the attribute and hides the series; keyboard focus keeps the library's focus ring.

<div class="legend">
    <button class="legend__item" type="button" aria-pressed="true">
        <span class="legend__swatch legend__swatch--line" style="--swatch-color: #1677ff"></span>
        Europe
    </button>
</div>
