## Conventions

- **Priority:** P0 (blocker) · P1 (important) · P2 (nice-to-have)
- **Type:** `bug` · `feat` · `a11y` · `perf` · `chore` · `docs` · `test` · `decision`
- **Estimate:** S (≤4 h) · M (1 day) · L (2–3 days) · XL (a week+)
- **IDs.** The `0.9.8_6` audit IDs stay as they are, because code comments, tests, `rare-charts.css` and the public CHANGELOG cite them. Families:
  - `R-` relation bugs (Graph flow, MapFlow)
  - `T-` time navigation and the navigator
  - `C-` older classes, theme and CSS
  - `K-` contracts and decisions before the 1.0 freeze
  - `D-` duplication
  - `X-` dead code
  - `S-` tests
  - `O-` docs and plans
  - `B-` owner requirements

  New tasks get `RC-NNN`. Rare Styles tasks are `CSS-NNN` in [`assets/css/docs/BACKLOG.md`](../styles/BACKLOG.md).
- **Pulled** marks work taken into a milestone from a later one because it touches the same code. It is the first thing to hand back if the milestone grows too large.

## Planning rules (owner decisions)

- **2026-11-17 — RareCharts stands on its own; Rare Styles only helps.** The library has its own styling: `core/theme.js` and `rare-charts.css`. It must look and work right in a minimal build with no Rare Styles on the page. No minimum Rare Styles version is declared. The guards against host styles stay, and a chart must not break on a Rare Styles `v0.7.1` host either. This answers RC-032 and the minimum-version question of section H.
- **2026-11-17 — the palette is the one agreed in Rare Styles** (`CSS-240`): #1677ff, #fa8c16, #00c97a, #ffcc00, #cc44ff, #ff0000.
  - #1677ff is the first series and the accent. #00aaff goes everywhere: the map's region fill and the link presets.
  - Orange stays only as the dark theme's accent.
  - The theme's literal values equal the palette, so a page without Rare Styles gets the same colours.
- **2026-11-17 — names, defaults and look (maintainer, answers to the prepared questions).**
  - **Motion (K-08).** One entry duration for every chart: 650 ms. Bars grow a little slower and the gauge fills a little faster than today. Graph transitions on a click stay at 500 ms.
  - **Defaults (K-10).**
    - Line and DualAxes date ticks follow the span, as TimeSeries and Bar do: `Jan 05`, `Jan`, `Jan ’24`, `2024` instead of `01/05`.
    - DualAxes without its own formatters labels its axes as Line does: `65k` and `0.5` instead of `65,000.00` and `+0.50`. The names `y1`/`y2` stay.
    - Everything else stays as it is and is recorded in the public defaults table (RC-033): area, Y ticks, curve, bar width ratio, gutters, centre label, node radius. TimeSeries keeps `'$'`, and its stroke and curve tension follow Line with D-09.
    - Bar's Y gutter goes from 65 to 64 px. An unknown curve name warns, and the two docs examples with a misspelt curve are fixed.
  - **Interactive legend (RC-054).**
    - A click toggles a series. A double click keeps only that series, and a second double click brings all back. A "Show all" item shows while anything is off. Graph works the same way (today its click keeps one type).
    - **A hidden series disappears, and the axes rescale to the series left** (the 2026-10-26 behaviour, confirmed after a short detour through dimming). The hidden series also leaves the tooltip, the crosshair and the end labels. `legendRescale: false` keeps the axes fixed.
  - **Map region clicks (B-06).** In this release the chart only tells the page which region was clicked; the page navigates itself. A region becomes focusable and shows a pointer only when a handler is set. Links from the data (`href`) come after 1.0.
  - **Fonts (K-15).** Words in a tooltip are set in Fira and numbers in Cousine, through one class. The navigator's time labels are Cousine at 10 px. No new size variables before 1.0.
  - **Tooltip line height: 24 px, as before** (v0.7.1), on any page. It is the chart's own value, not the host's.
  - **Tokens besides colours (RC-014).**
    - The tooltip size is the chart's own, and the size token is not read. It was 13 px; the maintainer chose 14 px with the new tooltip layout (2026-11-18, RC-082).
    - The font tokens and `--chart-focus-ring` are read with the chart's own fallbacks. Focus rings in Graph, Sankey and MapFlow turn blue on Rare Styles pages.
  - **Heights on a phone (RC-058).** TimeSeries: narrower range buttons in a narrow card, six on one row, which wins back its 38 px. Graph: +34 px at every width; the 20–27 px it still lacks on a phone is accepted.
  - **Chart header (RC-066).** The 460 px threshold stays. The buttons also move under the title when the title would be cut beside them: today TimeSeries at 600–800 px and the big flow at 600–900 px. Maintainer, 2026-11-18: and when the subtitle beside them runs past two lines (six at 700 px, three at 1000).
  - **Colours with no token (RC-064).**
    - Graph node fills and flow lane bands derive from the background and the text.
    - The map on a dark card uses the tokens.
    - The "+N" panel is drawn like the tooltip.
    - The grid and the tooltip border fall back to #e4e4e4, as in Rare Styles.
  - **Content starter.** Both pins (Rare Styles `v0.7.2`, RareCharts `0.9.8_7`) are bumped together right after the release, in a site commit of their own.
  - **Bar axes (maintainer, 2026-11-17, after D-10).** Category labels at the bottom thin like the time axis: every second (third…) one when neighbours would sit closer than 8 px. The value labels sit 8 px from the plot, as on Line (were 9).

- The files a lane owns (below) are off limits to the foreground and to the other lane in that wave. A foreground task that needs one waits for the merge.
- Only I edit `BACKLOG.md`, `overnight-2026-11-18-notes.md`, `DECISIONS.md` and the CHANGELOG. A lane reports its CHANGELOG lines, docs needs and decisions in its result.
- A lane asks nothing mid-run. An item that needs a decision stops there and goes into the report, and I ask the maintainer.
- Token economy (binding): tests of the touched files while working; the whole unit suite once at the end; only its own browser files; no mutation checks; the DOM oracle once at the end, only where the lane changes something visible.
